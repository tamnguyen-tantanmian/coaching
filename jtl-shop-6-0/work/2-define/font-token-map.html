Font token map

Seven text styles, each pointing at one of four font slots. This is the shared contract between Figma, the front end, and the theme panel — one row per thing, three names for it.

Structure

How it fits together

Layer 1

Font slots

Slot Figma variable FE token Default Referenced by (default)
Heading Font/Heading --font-heading Inter H1, H2
Subheading Font/Subheading --font-subheading Inter H3, H4
Body Font/Body --font-body Inter Paragraph, S1
Accent Font/Accent --font-accent Inter S2

A slot holds a family, not a weight. Regular / Semibold / Bold are pulled from whatever family sits in the slot, and a missing weight resolves upward (600 → 700) with a notice on the slot row. Never let the browser synthesise one.

Layer 2 · naming

Text styles — three names for one thing

Panel label Figma FE token prefix Default slot Applies to
H1 Type/H1 --type-h1-* Heading Hero headline · PDP product name · PLP category title
H2 Type/H2 --type-h2-* Heading Section title within a page
H3 Type/H3 --type-h3-* Subheading Product card title · accordion header · tab
H4 Type/H4 --type-h4-* Subheading Sub-section · filter group title
Paragraph Type/Paragraph --type-paragraph-* Body Product copy · CMS pages · input values
S1 · Price Type/S1-Price --type-price-* Body PDP price · cart total
S2 · Label Type/S2-Label --type-label-* Accent Badge · tag · overline · eyebrow

The panel label is what a merchant sees; the Figma and FE names are what the two teams type. S1 and S2 keep their number in the panel and their role in code — merchants read position, developers read meaning.

Fluid rule

min = base × 0.75 for base ≥ 24px; below 24 nothing scales. Interpolate between 360px and 1024px viewport. Paragraph, S1 and S2 hold their size at every width.

Why price scales differently

Price is a conversion element, not a heading. Shrinking it on the device where most traffic converts is the wrong trade, so it holds at 24.

Both price tokens carry font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums permanently. Without it, prices in a PLP grid don't align and the PDP price jitters when quantity changes.
